TEISOU KANA CARD BACKGROUNDS - NIGHT / DARK THEME
================================================================
2 prompts, one per card. Each block is complete and self-contained -
copy one whole block into Gemini.

Where they go:
  assets/banners/hiragana_card_dark.png
  assets/banners/katakana_card_dark.png

The code is already wired. `_KanaHeroCard` now picks the dark file in
dark mode and the existing light file otherwise, falling through to the
code-drawn `_KanaSceneryPainter` while a file is missing - so nothing
breaks before these exist, and nothing needs changing once they do.
Drop both in and rebuild.


MATCH THE DAYTIME CARDS - LOOK AT THEM FIRST
--------------------------------------------
Open assets/banners/hiragana_card.png and katakana_card.png before
generating. These are night twins of those two, not new scenes.

  hiragana_card.png - a very pale coral-pink wash. Mount Fuji in the
  middle, a cherry blossom branch reaching in from the left edge, and a
  red torii gate standing on the right. Everything extremely faint,
  fading to near-white at the top.

  katakana_card.png - the same idea in pale blue. A small Mount Fuji
  left of centre, a five-storey pagoda on the right, a pale sun disc
  high on the left, and soft mist. Again very faint, fading to
  near-white at the top.

The two must stay visibly a pair: same style, same framing, same
weight - only the palette and the landmark change.


THESE ARE BACKGROUNDS, NOT PICTURES
-----------------------------------
This is the thing to get right. Each one sits *behind* the card's own
text, so it must stay quiet:

  - top-left holds a coloured circle icon (あ / ア) with a title and a
    subtitle beneath it
  - bottom-right holds a round arrow button

So keep both of those corners nearly empty - no landmark, no branch, no
detail there. Put the interest in the middle and along the lower edge,
exactly as the daytime versions do.

Low contrast throughout. If the art competes with the text, it is
wrong, however pretty it is. The daytime cards are so faint they are
almost a tint, and the night versions should be equally restrained
against their darker ground.


TECHNICAL
---------
Request 1200x645 (about 1.86:1, the daytime cards' exact ratio).

CROPPING - this was got wrong once, read it carefully.

The card is TALLER than it is wide (about 183x210 dp), while this image
is wide (1.86:1). BoxFit.cover therefore crops the SIDES, not the top
and bottom: **only the middle 47% of the image width is ever seen.**

So the landmark - the torii, the pagoda - has to sit near the
horizontal centre, not out at an edge. The first night pair put the
pagoda at the right edge, exactly where the light version has it, and
it vanished entirely: both dark cards ended up showing nothing but Mt.
Fuji and were indistinguishable from each other. That file had to be
re-cropped by hand onto its pagoda afterwards.

Put the landmark in the middle. Mt. Fuji and the landmark cannot both
survive - in the light pair they do not either (the hiragana card shows
Fuji, the katakana card shows the pagoda), so pick one per card and
centre it.

Do NOT ask for a transparent background. Generators draw the
transparency checkerboard as real pixels and it ships into the app;
that has happened on this project before. Paint the night sky solid.

Each card sits on a solid colour that the art must blend into at the
edges rather than cut against:
  hiragana card ground  #34232B  (deep plum)
  katakana card ground  #1F2838  (deep navy blue)

No text, no letters, no kana, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no
frame or border, no UI elements. The app draws its own icon and title
on top.


==============================================
1. hiragana_card_dark.png  (plum / coral night)
==============================================
A soft, extremely subtle illustrated background for a card in a children's Japanese-learning app, in a gentle kawaii pastel style - flat, simple, softly shaded, no photorealism and no fine busy detail. This image sits behind text, so it must read as a quiet tint rather than as a picture competing for attention.

Scene: a calm night landscape in Japan, seen as a wide horizontal strip. Mount Fuji sits in the middle distance, centred, a soft rounded silhouette with a faint pale snow cap. A cherry blossom branch reaches in from the left edge with a few soft blossoms. On the right, a simple traditional torii gate stands in silhouette, its familiar shape clear but its detail minimal. A handful of blossom petals drift softly through the air.

Palette and mood: a deep plum night. The background is a dark warm purple-brown around #34232B, deepening slightly toward the upper edge. Everything in the scene is rendered only a little lighter than that ground - dusty muted rose and soft mauve, around #FF8296 heavily desaturated and dimmed - so the whole image stays within a narrow, low-contrast range. Moonlit and calm, warm rather than cold. The torii is the deepest accent, a slightly richer muted red, but still soft and never bright.

Composition: keep the upper-left corner and the lower-right corner almost empty and free of detail - the app places an icon and a title in one and a button in the other. Mount Fuji and the torii belong in the middle and lower-middle of the frame. The very top of the image should fade toward the flat background colour so the card's edge is invisible.

Very low contrast overall. No harsh highlights, no glow, no strong light source, no stars bright enough to draw the eye.

No text, no letters, no kana characters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no UI elements, no frame or border, no transparent background.
