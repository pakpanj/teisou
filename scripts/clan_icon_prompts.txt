TEISOU CLAN ICON PROMPTS - KAMON (JAPANESE CREST) STYLE, PLAIN TEXT
=====================================================================
20 ready-to-paste prompts, one per clan icon preset. Each block below is
a complete, self-contained prompt (style + motif already merged) - copy
one whole block at a time straight into Gemini.

Style: a traditional Japanese kamon (family crest) redrawn as a flat,
bold, two-tone silhouette badge - solid pastel field colour behind, a
single solid silhouette colour for the motif, no gradients/shading,
strongly symmetric, thick dark-plum outline ring. Not the harsh black-
and-white of a real mon - the app's own soft pastel palette instead.

After generating: each image comes out on a flat magenta background
(#FF00DC) outside the circle - that is intentional, so it is easy to
spot and erase. Remove it manually with any background-removal tool
(Photopea, GIMP "select by colour" then delete, remove.bg, or Gemini's
own edit feature), then save the result square (1024x1024 recommended)
directly as assets/clan_icons/{id}.png - no script needed, the app
picks the art up automatically once a PNG with the matching filename
appears there.

==============================================
A traditional Japanese kamon (family/clan crest) rendered as a flat circular emblem - bold, solid, highly symmetrical silhouette artwork, centred inside a circular badge that fills most of the square canvas. Think of a real kamon medallion (a stencil-like, single-colour crest silhouette on a plain background) redrawn in a soft pastel palette instead of the traditional black-on-white.

The badge is two-tone, exactly like a real kamon: the circle's interior is one solid soft pastel colour (its "field"), and the motif sits on top as a single solid silhouette shape in ONE contrasting pastel-friendly colour - no gradients inside the motif, no internal shading, no multi-colour illustration. Pick colours from a warm pastel palette (coral, cream, soft blue, soft green, soft gold) for the field, and a deeper but still soft tone (dusty rose, muted teal, warm ochre, dusty plum) for the motif silhouette, so the two read clearly apart.

The circle is outlined with a thick, even dark-plum / maroon line (not black). This is the only "line" in the whole image; the motif itself must be a filled silhouette, not an outlined drawing.

Interior motif: a Shinto shrine torii gate silhouette, seen straight-on - two upright pillars and two horizontal crossbars, already naturally symmetric left-to-right, rendered as one flat solid silhouette shape.

Style: absolutely flat solid silhouette shape only - no gradient, no shading, no texture, no photorealism, no internal detail lines cutting through the silhouette (real kamon achieve detail only through the outline of the shape itself, like a paper cut-out or a rubber stamp, never by drawing lines on top of a filled area). Strong bilateral or radial symmetry, the defining trait of a real kamon. Bold and legible at small sizes - simplify toward a clean geometric silhouette rather than an illustrated scene. Represent the motif accurately and respectfully as a real Japanese cultural object/symbol - simplified into crest form, but recognisable, not a generic invented shape.

Composition: square 1024x1024 image, circular badge centred with a small even margin, everything OUTSIDE the circle solid flat magenta #FF00DC background (this will be removed afterward, keep it perfectly flat and uniform). No text, no letters, no watermark, no border around the whole canvas, no drop shadow.
