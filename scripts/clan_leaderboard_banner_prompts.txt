TEISOU CLAN LEADERBOARD BANNER PROMPTS - SAKURA, DAY + NIGHT
================================================================
2 prompts, one per theme mode. Each is complete and self-contained -
copy one whole block into Gemini.

Where these go: assets/leaderboard/clan_banner_light.png and
assets/leaderboard/clan_banner_dark.png (paths TBD when this is wired
up in code - not built yet, prompts only for now). The app will swap
between them automatically based on the user's light/dark theme.

Size: request 1600x900 (16:9). Don't worry about exact final crop -
Flutter will fit/crop it into the actual banner area in code.

After generating: just hand both PNGs back and they'll get a small
edge-colour touch-up (nudging the very top/bottom pixels to exactly
match the app's real background colour, #FFFFFF for light / #121620
for dark) so the seam against the surrounding screen is invisible -
no background removal needed this time, unlike the circular clan
icons, since this is a full rectangular scene meant to sit flush
against the page background rather than a cutout badge.

1. clan_banner_light.png (day / light theme)
==============================================
A wide, gentle illustrated banner scene for a Japanese-learning app, in a soft kawaii pastel style - flat cel-shaded illustration with soft simple shading, no photorealism, no busy fine detail (this sits behind UI text and controls, so it needs to stay calm and uncluttered, not compete with them).

Scene: a peaceful daytime sakura (cherry blossom) landscape, seen as a wide horizontal strip. In the foreground, on the left, a few cherry blossom branches reach in from the edge with soft pink blossoms and a handful of petals drifting through the air. In the background, gentle layered misty mountains recede into the distance (each layer a slightly lighter, softer blue-grey than the one in front of it, classic soft atmospheric perspective). Sky: a soft pale blue, warm and gentle, like a calm clear late-morning - no harsh sun glare, no dramatic clouds.

Colour and mood: warm, soft, and friendly pastel palette throughout (dusty pink blossoms, soft sage-green foliage, powder-blue misty mountains, soft gold-cream light) - the same gentle, never-harsh pastel language used throughout this app's other art, never neon, never high-contrast.

Critical requirement - the top and bottom edges of the image: the scene must fade/blend into a perfectly flat, solid, uniform white (#FFFFFF) at the very top edge and the very bottom edge of the image - like the illustration is gently emerging from a plain white page at both ends, not a hard rectangular frame or vignette border. The flat white band at the very top and bottom should be genuinely solid and uniform right at the edge (a clean, calm fade, not a gradient that never quite reaches pure white) - this image will sit directly on a plain white app background with no further masking, so the blend has to be seamless on its own.

Composition: 1600x900 landscape, horizontal scene, calm and readable at a glance. No text, no letters, no watermark, no hard border or frame around the canvas, no drop shadow.
